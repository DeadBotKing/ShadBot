\# After Phase 12



The Agent Platform is considered complete.



No architectural development continues.



From this point forward:



\- Only Product Workspaces are created.

\- Tasks are placed inside:



```

Project Workspace/

└── Tasks/

&#x20;   └── task.md

```



The platform then:



\- Reads the task

\- Understands the goal

\- Builds the plan

\- Executes agents

\- Validates output

\- Learns

\- Reports completion



No additional platform features are required for normal product development.

\# Roadmap Rules



1\. Complete phases sequentially.

2\. Never skip a phase.

3\. Every phase must satisfy its completion criteria.

4\. Architecture changes are forbidden after Phase 12.

5\. Product development begins only after Platform Freeze v1.0.
